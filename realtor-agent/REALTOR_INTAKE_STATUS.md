## Confirm before using the 20% rule

The sheet says purchase price ≤ **20% of ARV**.

That is not the same as the common investor rule “20% **below** ARV” (buy at 80% of ARV).

Example on a $400,000 ARV house:

- **As written (20% of ARV):** max offer about **$80,000**
- **If he meant 20% below ARV:** max offer about **$320,000**

Ask him to circle one. Do not guess. Matching and scoring cannot go live on this profile until that is confirmed.

Also ask: where does ARV come from? MLS does not ship a reliable ARV. Options: he types it, his investor types it, a designated estimator, or “don’t match on ARV until we have a number.”
